# kintsugi-cga

The 2D Conformal Geometric Algebra (CGA) engine at the heart of Kintsugi: geometric primitives, operators (join, meet, dot, ...), and versor-based transforms in R(3,1), implemented from scratch in pure Kotlin. No external dependencies.

## Math Resources

The algebra used in Kintsugi follows [Eric Lengyel](https://x.com/EricLengyel)'s formulation of CGA, including its basis, null metric, operator names and sign conventions.

[**Projective Geometric Algebra Illuminated**](https://projectivegeometricalgebra.org/) (2024) covers it in full, from wedge and antiwedge, bulk and weight and the geometric antiproduct through to motors, flectors and round objects.

The rest of his work (wikis, a C++ library, Mathematica packages) is gathered at [projectivegeometricalgebra.org](https://projectivegeometricalgebra.org/).
